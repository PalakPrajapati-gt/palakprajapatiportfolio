UI/UX CASE STUDY

PetBloom – A Mobile App for Pet Health & Wellness

I designed the PetBloom mobile app UI in Figma, focusing on a clean, user-friendly experience. The app offers personalized product recommendations and a cozy design to make pet wellness shopping simple and enjoyable.

💡 Design Challenges & Smart Solutions

🚧 Problem

Pet owners often face difficulty finding reliable, health-focused products in one place. The experience is scattered and lacks personalization.

✅ Solution

PetBloom is a mobile app designed to simplify pet wellness shopping. It offers curated products, personalized recommendations, and an easy, comforting experience for pet parents.

  • Browse natural, vet-approved products
  • Filter by pet type, age & needs
  • Create pet profiles & save favorites
